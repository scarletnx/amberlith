# The demos' four tracks: what each is made of, for the composer (musiccompose.c) to build a song from a
# seed, played by the FM synthesizer in music.c as an AdLib tracker plays its nine channels.
# tools/music-score.py turns this file into source/musicscore.c; tools/test_music_score.py holds the
# two to each other.
#
# A track: its key and mode, tempo (quarter notes a minute; a row is a sixteenth, a bar 16 rows) and
# ticks a row (when slides and volume slides move), gain (in 32nds), what each of the nine channels plays
# (drums: the whole kit on one channel; bass; pad, up to three; lead; echo, the lead some rows late and
# softer; double, the lead again drifting sharp, at the climax; harmony, a third under the lead, in the
# B section and the climax; arp, an ostinato; crash, the cymbal and the riser), the patches of each voice
# (music.c's, by name; two, split by a comma, for the seed to pick from), the ranges its parts keep to,
# as keyed (a patch's multiple moves what sounds), the echo's rows and how much softer it is, and the
# pads' glide into each new chord (32nds of a semitone a tick; 0 strikes it again).
#
# Then its material, one entry a line, as many as it likes; a seed picks from each:
#   form       sections in order: I intro, A, B, R breakdown, C climax (the A melody, doubled)
#   home       a progression of four bars, a scale degree each (1 to 7), for intro, A and climax
#   lift       the same, for the B section
#   drop       the same, for the breakdown
#   groove     16 rows of drums: K kick, S snare, s a soft snare, h hat, H a loud hat, . nothing
#   fill       the drums of a section's last bar
#   intro      the drums of an intro's first four bars
#   breakbeat  the drums of a breakdown, no kick
#   bass       16 rows: R root, O its octave, 3 5 7 the third, fifth and seventh above, A a step
#              into the next bar's root; lower case glides into the note; - rings on, . let go
#   breakbass  the bass of a breakdown
#   pad        16 rows of the pads' chord: x struck, - held, . let go
#   climax     the pads at the climax: c the next note of a cascade, each pad in turn
#   arp        16 rows: 1 to 9 a step up the scale from the chord's root, - rings on, . let go
#   rhythm     32 rows of the lead (two bars): x a note, - held, . a rest

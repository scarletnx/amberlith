# The demos' four tracks, written note by note for the FM synthesizer in music.c: nine channels in the
# OPL3's manner, bass, three chord voices, the lead (or the counter-melody, which plays where the lead
# rests), arpeggio, kick, snare and hats. tools/music-score.py turns this file into source/musicscore.c;
# tools/test_music_score.py holds the two to each other.
#
# A track: its key, tempo (beats a minute; a beat is four steps in 4/4 and six in 12/8), steps a bar,
# swing (how far the second step of each pair comes late, in 64ths of a step), the instruments of its
# parts (music.c's patches by name), its gain (in 32nds), its form (sections in the order they play;
# the loop comes back to the second), each section's chords (one a bar), its lead and counter-melody
# (NAME OCTAVE:STEPS, r:STEPS a rest; a ~ after the length glides into the note), and the patterns its
# bass, drums, chords and arpeggio play (by name, `fill` putting each pattern's fill on the last bar).
#
# Patterns are grids, a character a step. Bass: R root, 3 third, 5 fifth, 7 seventh, 8 octave, A a step
# below the next bar's root (glided into), - held, . silent. Drums, two lanes of one name: K kick, S
# snare, s a soft snare; and hats: h closed, H a loud one, o open. Chords: X struck, - held, . off.
# Arpeggio: 1 to 6 the chord's notes from the root (root, third, fifth, octave, its third, its fifth),
# . none.
